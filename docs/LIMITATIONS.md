# Current limitations

- The project is unofficial and not endorsed by ETS.
- Bundled content is original and structurally aligned, not operational TOEFL content.
- Reading routing is uncalibrated and cannot reproduce ETS operational adaptivity.
- No result is converted into an official 1–6 score.
- Writing estimates require a configured LLM and still need human-scored benchmark validation.
- Formal records are browser-local; there is no account or cross-device synchronization layer.
- Only Reading and Writing are implemented.
- The bundled bank is adequate for a controlled pilot, not large-scale exposure control.
- Public deployment requires external authentication and additional security controls.
